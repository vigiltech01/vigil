Your FortiGate wrote millions of log lines today.

How many of them did anyone actually look at? 👀

Attackers count on that. So we built something to fix it.

Meet **Vigil**, by Vigil F·Tech (Vigil Firewall Tech). It turns your FortiGate's raw syslog into something you can *see*:

🔵 Every inbound request becomes a particle, live, in 3D, flying in from its country of origin
🔴 Colour-coded by what the firewall decided: allowed, closed port, source not allowed, security block, IPS exploit
❄️ Freeze time and click any single request: the rule, NAT, application, client reputation and the original log line
🛡️ Every internet-facing rule graded 0–100, with known exploited CVEs (CISA KEV) and the exact FortiOS commands to fix it
⚡ Disable a rule on the firewall and the score updates within seconds, straight from the FortiGate's own change log
🔎 Ask in plain English: "why was this IP blocked?" and follow the request hop by hop

And setup takes about a minute:
✅ One Docker container
✅ One CLI block on the firewall
✅ No agent, no API keys, no cloud account
✅ Your logs never leave your network

It's free and open source (Apache-2.0).

We're a small team building open-source security tools, and this is our first one. We'd love your honest feedback, especially if you run FortiGates, a NOC/SOC or an MSP.

👉 Link to the GitHub repo and the one-line demo in the first comment.

What's the one question you wish your firewall logs could answer instantly? 👇

## Tips for maximum reach

- **Native video + no link in the post body.** Put the link in the first comment; posts with outside links often get
  less reach.
- **Post Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 am** in your audience's main time zone, and stay around for the first hour: reply to
  every comment, since early engagement drives distribution.
- **Reshare from your personal profile** with one personal line ("Here's what I've been building…"). Personal posts
  usually reach far more people than company pages.
- **Ask 5–10 people who care** (colleagues, FortiGate admins you know) to comment with a real opinion, not just a like.
- **Don't tag Fortinet** or imply a partnership. Vigil is independent, and the video's end card says so.
- **Keep 3–5 hashtags**; more tends to look spammy.
- A day later, post a follow-up with one feature (e.g. the "freeze time" clip) and link back to the launch post.
